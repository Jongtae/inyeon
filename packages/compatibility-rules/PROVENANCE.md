## Authority boundary

This package is infrastructure evidence, not Saju methodology evidence. Its sources are Issues #33 and #34, ADR 0010, ADR 0015, ADR 0017, ADR 0018, Decisions D-0002 and D-0003, and repository governance. These sources justify inspectable deterministic architecture, categorical input-availability semantics, and safety constraints; they do not authorize a traditional interpretation.

Qualified cultural/Saju review, attributable rule-specific references, approved fixtures, and upstream chart production validation are required before a mapping can be approved or exposed. The product catalog therefore remains empty. Test-only synthetic rules are explicitly non-methodological and are not package exports or product data.

## Privacy and determinism

Runtime accepts derived in-memory chart features only. It accepts no raw birth date, time, place, identity, relationship structure, free-form expression, callback, prompt, or LLM output. The public snapshot omits raw chart facts. The implementation performs no network, persistence, URL, cache, console, clock, randomness, or locale-dependent operation.

`evidence-availability-v1` is candidate infrastructure. Its categorical states and limitation codes express input and variant coverage under the selected methodology; they do not express scientific validity, predictive probability, relationship quality, or a ranking score. The model was verified with synthetic test-only rules because the product catalog contains no approved rules. It creates no new Saju claim.
