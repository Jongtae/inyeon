# Trust & Safety Baseline

Dating safety is P0 infrastructure, not a post-launch feature.

## Minimum launch capabilities

- 18+ gate and age-policy enforcement
- report, block, unmatch
- profile/photo moderation
- spam/scam/catfish controls
- rate limits and abuse throttling
- safety center and user guidance
- moderation case workflow
- audit trail for enforcement actions
- escalation path for severe threats and credible imminent-harm reports
- location fuzzing; never expose exact coordinates
- support contact and response ownership

## Product rules

- Compatibility must never be presented as evidence a person is safe or trustworthy.
- Safety exclusions override recommendation/ranking.
- Verification status may inform trust UI, but do not overclaim identity guarantees.
- Do not charge for core safety controls.
- Do not use astrology to infer violence, criminality, mental illness, fertility, sexual behavior, morality, or trustworthiness.

## Release gate

No public beta until report/block/unmatch flows work end-to-end and a human moderation operating path exists.
