# INYEON Business Plan

Status: working business hypothesis for US launch.

## 1. Business thesis

INYEON is not a horoscope utility with dating added. It is a dating marketplace whose differentiation is an authentically Korean, explainable relationship lens.

Positioning:

> **INYEON — Dating through the Korean idea of connection.**
>
> Meet people first. Understand why you may fit second.

The service should own the question:

> **Why might we work?**

not a numeric “How compatible are we?” score.

## 2. Competitive position

The company operates in whitespace between:

- **Hinge** — intentional dating, prompts, date feedback, outcome learning;
- **Tinder** — large marketplace, AI-curated discovery, Western astrology features;
- **The Pattern** — relationship dynamics and Bonds;
- **Co–Star** — astrology/self and relationship interpretation;
- **CHANI** — astrology + reflection;
- niche astrology/dating products that often rely on zodiac-based filtering or scores.

INYEON’s differentiated stack:

1. Korean cultural identity and vocabulary;
2. deterministic Saju/Four Pillars calculation;
3. transparent, structured compatibility explanations;
4. no public pseudo-precision compatibility score;
5. empirical outcome measurement from real dating behavior;
6. Couple Mode after successful matching.

“Uses your birthday to find matches” is not a moat by itself.

## 3. Target market entry

Initial ICP:

- US adults 23–38 as acquisition focus;
- relationship-oriented;
- culturally curious / K-culture adjacent;
- comfortable with reflective compatibility frameworks;
- LGBTQ-inclusive.

Important subsegments:

- K-culture native users;
- Korean-American / diaspora users;
- astrology-curious daters;
- intentional daters tired of swipe volume;
- compatibility skeptics who value transparency and calibration;
- couples as a post-dating lifecycle.

Do not limit the service to Korean-Americans. Their participation is especially important for authenticity and word-of-mouth, but the business opportunity is broader cultural export.

## 4. Brand and cultural strategy

Working brand: **INYEON / 인연**.

Trademark, domain, app-store name, and social-handle clearance remain required.

Brand rule:

> **English tells users what it means; Korean tells users where it comes from.**

Use genuine Korean concepts selectively rather than applying generic K-pop/K-drama visual clichés.

Cultural framing:

> Korean Saju and Gung-hap are Korean practices within the broader East Asian Four Pillars tradition.

Avoid claiming that Korea exclusively invented the underlying system.

## 5. Acquisition wedge

The launch campaign should sell **Inyeon**, not “AI astrology.”

Message examples:

> Maybe you do not need more matches. Maybe you need more context.

> You have tried zodiac compatibility. Korea has another way of looking at relationships.

> No soulmate scores. Just another way to understand why two people might click—or clash.

Priority channels:

- culturally credible Korean-American / K-culture creators;
- TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts;
- dating/relationship creators;
- campus Korean student and Asian-American organizations;
- Korean restaurants/cafés/bookstores/cultural venues;
- K-town events and “Gung-hap Night” concepts;
- referral/share cards that preserve privacy.

## 6. Cold-start strategy

Dating marketplace quality is local.

Default approach:

1. choose one metro;
2. recruit a balanced seed population before broad opening;
3. use invite-only cohorts and physical/community partnerships;
4. watch eligible-candidate density by orientation/intent cohort;
5. open gradually;
6. delay second-city expansion until first-city liquidity is healthy.

Provisional city recommendation: **Los Angeles**.

Alternative: NYC if founding/community distribution can seed a denser, more balanced verified pool faster.

Launch gate:

> approximately **2,000 genuinely eligible, balanced, verified profiles** before broad launch.

Then use staged targets such as ~10k MAU pilot and ~50k MAU scale only after quality works locally.

## 7. Retention loop

Dating apps normally lose users when they succeed. INYEON should intentionally extend the lifecycle:

`discovery → match → chat → date → relationship → Couple Mode`

Retention mechanisms:

- Inyeon Match;
- post-match Gung-hap reports;
- date questions;
- post-date feedback;
- Couple Mode weekly/monthly prompts;
- optional shared relationship cycles;
- couple subscription independent of dating discovery.

The product must still celebrate successful exit from the dating marketplace; Couple Mode is opt-in, not a dark retention mechanism.

## 8. Monetization hypotheses

These are testable starting points, not fixed pricing.

| Offer | Starting hypothesis | Value |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | $0 | core dating, chat, basic compatibility card, limited Inyeon Match |
| Inyeon Plus | $14.99/mo or ~$89/yr | full Gung-hap, more curation, advanced non-sensitive filters, rewinds |
| Inyeon Premium | $24.99/mo or ~$149/yr | deeper reports, priority curated experience, premium Couple content |
| Gung-hap Pass | $6.99 one-off | deep report for two consenting profiles |
| Couple Pass | ~$39–49/yr/couple | standalone Couple Mode |
| Founder Beta | ~$59–79/yr | early-adopter annual plan |

Never monetize:

- blocking;
- reporting;
- harassment escape;
- basic privacy controls;
- safety-critical verification.

Subscription copy, renewal, cancellation, and refund flows must be plain and compliant.

## 9. Unit economics hypotheses

Do not optimize CAC/LTV before local retention and liquidity are measurable.

Track acquisition economics at these levels:

- CAC per signup;
- CAC per completed profile;
- CAC per verified active user;
- CAC per meaningful connection;
- paid CAC by channel;
- 30/90-day contribution by cohort;
- referral-adjusted CAC.

The most useful early denominator is **verified active user**, not raw install.

LTV models should be scenario-based until there is real churn/ARPPU data.

## 10. Financial scenarios

Planning estimates from the research synthesis, not vendor quotes.

### Lean founder-led plan

- controlled alpha: roughly **$120k–$250k**;
- 12-month path: roughly **$450k–$900k**.

Assumptions:

- one city;
- single mobile codebase;
- managed services;
- hand-curated rule library;
- modest but real moderation and verification;
- no advanced ML before measurement maturity.

### Properly staffed first year

Approximate range: **$1.5M–$3.3M**.

Planning allocation:

- product/engineering/design: $850k–$1.5M;
- trust & safety/moderation: $180k–$390k;
- legal/privacy/insurance: $90k–$220k;
- Saju experts/cultural research: $45k–$100k;
- cloud/LLM/vendor tooling: $60k–$180k;
- verification: $45k–$135k;
- research/community: $75k–$180k;
- launch/growth/events: $300k–$760k.

The company should not extend runway by removing legal/privacy architecture, authentication, moderation, or basic safety.

## 11. 6 / 12 / 24 month business scenarios

These are planning scenarios to replace with observed data.

### 6 months — city beta

Downside:

- <3k MAU;
- limited cohort liquidity;
- free product only or minimal founder plan;
- spend focused on research/safety/product.

Base:

- 5k–10k MAU in one metro;
- 2k+ seeded verified profiles;
- early paid conversion experiment;
- measurable meaningful-conversation funnel.

Upside:

- 15k+ MAU with healthy cohort liquidity;
- creator/referral loop emerging;
- paid conversion strong enough to justify broader acquisition.

### 12 months — PMF search

Downside:

- remain single-city;
- reduce growth spend;
- iterate on matching/context proposition.

Base:

- ~25k–50k MAU across one strong city and controlled second-city test;
- subscription + Couple Mode revenue;
- compatibility experiment has enough power to separate presentation vs matching effect.

Upside:

- >50k MAU;
- repeatable creator/community acquisition;
- second city liquid;
- meaningful organic/referral contribution.

### 24 months — expansion decision

Scale only if:

- safety rates remain acceptable;
- retention survives novelty decay;
- cohorts have healthy marketplace liquidity;
- acquisition can be repeated city-by-city;
- Gung-hap materially improves context and/or outcomes;
- Couple Mode or premium provides credible monetization.

Avoid national expansion based only on press, virality, or top-of-funnel installs.

## 14. Major failure modes

### Astrology gimmick

Risk: users try it once, then churn.

Mitigation: dating utility first, transparent methodology, outcomes measured beyond Likes.

### Cultural flattening / fetishization

Risk: product feels like a generic dating app wrapped in Korean aesthetics.

Mitigation: real Korean terminology, advisors, diaspora testing, plain-English meaning, no stereotypes.

### Cold start

Risk: insufficient compatible supply by cohort.

Mitigation: one-city launch, pre-seeding, waitlist, invite-only rollout, community partners.

### Safety incident

Risk: existential trust damage.

Mitigation: block/report/moderation/verification from MVP, strong escalation and incident response.

### High CAC

Risk: marketplace requires expensive continuous acquisition.

Mitigation: creator/community/event wedge, referral loops, city density before scale.

### Saju calculation error

Risk: credibility collapse.

Mitigation: deterministic engine, methodology versioning, ≥200 golden fixtures, expert review.

### AI hallucination

Risk: harmful/fatalistic relationship claims.

Mitigation: structured rule facts only, no raw chart calculation in LLM, schema/banned-claim validator.

### Privacy breach

Risk: severe harm because data includes dating preferences, orientation, location, birth details, messages, verification.

Mitigation: segregation, minimization, encryption, no raw PII in analytics/LLM, tested deletion, audit logging.

## 15. Go / Conditional Go / No-Go

Current business recommendation: **Conditional Go**.

Conditions:

1. prove concept comprehension and trust with US target users;
2. validate a culturally credible compatibility taxonomy with Korean Saju advisors;
3. validate deterministic chart correctness;
4. seed one city to real liquidity;
5. show Gung-hap improves conversation quality or durable user value without worsening safety/fairness;
6. keep privacy and trust & safety as launch requirements rather than deferred polish.

The most dangerous unproven assumption is not whether people find Saju interesting. It is whether **that interest produces better dating behavior and retention after novelty wears off**.
