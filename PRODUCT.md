# Product Definition

## Positioning

**INYEON — Dating through the Korean idea of connection.**

INYEON is a dating product first. Korean Saju and Gung-hap provide an explainable cultural compatibility layer after real-world eligibility and safety constraints.

The product answers:

> **Why might we work — and what should we talk about?**

not:

> **What percentage soulmates are we?**

## Initial target

US-first, adult (18+) users, initially one dense metro rather than nationwide. Focus on relationship-oriented and culturally curious users while remaining broadly inclusive.

## Core experience

1. Account + 18+ gate
2. Intent, preferences, deal-breakers
3. Dating profile
4. Private birth inputs: date, birthplace, optional time
5. Deterministic Saju chart
6. Discovery with bounded Gung-hap context
7. Limited curated `Inyeon Match`
8. Like → mutual match → chat
9. Gung-hap relationship explanation with “what clicks / watch for / question to ask”
10. Block/report/unmatch and safety center
11. Optional post-date outcome feedback
12. Couple Mode after mutual consent

## UX rules

- Public profile remains recognizably a dating profile.
- Compatibility explanations appear as context, not authority.
- Traditional terms are progressive disclosure.
- Birth time is optional. Unknown time must not be silently substituted.
- No public numeric score.
- Negative language must describe dynamics, never moral worth or inevitability.

## Success metric

Primary product-quality metric:

**Meaningful Connections per 100 Verified Weekly Active Users**

Initial proxy: mutual match + both users message + reciprocal conversation threshold.

Later: self-reported date + optional “want to meet again.”
